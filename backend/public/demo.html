Sire Printing

Demo page. The chat bubble in the bottom-right is the embeddable Sire Printing assistant widget.

Try it: open the bubble, then use the phone icon for a hands-free voice call, or the document icon to open the callback form.

How the embed works

On a real site you paste this once, just before </body>:

<script src="https://chat.yourdomain.com/widget.js"
        data-sireprinting-id="default"
        data-title="Sire Printing"
        data-accent="#4f46e5"
        data-position="br"
        async></script>

You can also control it from your own buttons: SirePrinting.open(), SirePrinting.close(), SirePrinting.toggle().
